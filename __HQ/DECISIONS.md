# DECISIONS — settled calls, do not relitigate

Locked design/architecture decisions — **one line each: the choice + a one-line WHY**. Owned by the
**Plan** role. Read this BEFORE (re)designing, so you don't reopen a closed question or re-derive a
solved one.

- **What goes in:** only genuinely-settled, deliberated calls — not every small choice.
- **When you write:** the moment "ok, decided X" happens. Append a line; keep the WHY — it's what is
  lost first, and a fresh/weak model has no memory of the debate.
- **When you read:** Plan role before designing; anyone tempted to change something fundamental.
- **Reversing a locked call** is a conscious move (fresh rationale), not a casual edit — update the line.

This is NOT Vision (how it *should* be) nor Doc (how it *works now*) — it is the greppable index of
closed doors plus the reason the alternative lost.

Format: `- <decision> — <one-line why>`
